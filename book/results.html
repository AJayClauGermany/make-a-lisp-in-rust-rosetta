  • rapidhash: reaches stepA_mal; rapidhash 4.5.1. std HashMap with rapidhash's GlobalState (one seed per process) per scope

Stacks

A stack is a whole interpreter built from one row of each component and compiled as one unit, so these numbers include what the compiler made of the combination. Each stack is timed on the evaluation programs and the REPL session, and compared with the all-std stack. Ratio is the geometric mean of its time over the std stack's; for a stack of several non-std rows, predicted multiplies the ratios of the stacks that swap in each row alone, and interaction is ratio over predicted: below 1× the rows do better together than apart, above 1× worse. Interactions within 5% are marked as noise. See Stacks for how to run other combinations.
