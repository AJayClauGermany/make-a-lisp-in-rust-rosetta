Make-a-Lisp in Rust: a rosetta comparison

Environment

Trait: mal_traits::Env. Directory: comparisons/env/.

An environment maps symbols to values and chains to an outer environment for lexical scoping. Closures capture environments, so environments are shared and cheap to clone. This component compares map crates and persistent data-structure crates, and is sensitive to allocation count and lookup speed on short string keys.

What the job demands

  • child() creates an empty scope that falls back to its parent.
  • set() binds in the innermost scope only.
  • get() walks outward; the evaluator turns None into a 'x' not found error.

How the rows are measured

Each row is timed twice: the trait methods on their own (set, local and outer lookups, a miss, child), and the std evaluator running the PROGRAMS workloads on that environment, which is where binding parameters and walking scopes show up in practice.

The persistent maps (imbl, the maintained fork of im, and rpds) cannot be used the way they are meant to be: Env::set through a parent has to be visible to children created before it, which rules out giving each child a structurally shared snapshot. Both rows keep the scope chain and a RefCell like the others. See Trait notes.
