Make-a-Lisp in Rust: a rosetta comparison

Reader

Trait: mal_traits::Reader. Directory: comparisons/reader/.

The reader tokenises source text on the mal token grammar and builds lists, vectors, hash-maps, strings, keywords, numbers and symbols, including the reader macros ', `, ~, ~@, @ and ^. It is the component with the richest field of candidate crates, and the one most directly comparable to parse-rosetta-rs.

What the job demands

  • Unbalanced delimiters and bad string escapes must be reported as errors, not panics; the test suite checks the messages loosely.
  • Blank and comment-only input is ReadOutcome::Empty, not an error.
  • Only the first top-level form is read; trailing input is ignored.

Notes from the rows

Every grammar crate had to be bent to read one form and ignore the rest, and only lalrpop and chumsky report running out of input in words the test suite accepts on their own; the others decide it from the error position. chumsky's parser type borrows the input, so it is rebuilt on every read_str call and its timings include that. See Trait notes for the details and proposed refinements.
